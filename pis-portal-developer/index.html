Guides & frequently asked questions

How do I try the demo?

Search DEMO-001, enter an amount from RM 1.00 to RM 1,200.00, review it, then simulate the payment. Reset to try again.

Does this check a real account or make a payment?

No. All account values and receipts are fictional. No data is sent to a server.

What does this project demonstrate?

Responsive interface structure, form validation, a guarded multi-step flow, currency formatting and safe rendering of page content.
